# AIRBNB-SALES-DASHBOARD
This project analyzes Airbnb listing and booking data to uncover insights around revenue performance, host behavior, listing quality, and booking efficiency across multiple cities.

The goal was not just to visualize numbers, but to translate data into actionable business decisions that could improve conversion, trust, and overall platform performance.

The dashboard provides a multi-dimensional view of Airbnb operations, covering sales trends, listing attributes, host responsiveness, cleanliness ratings, pricing perception, and booking behavior.

## 🎯 Objectives of the Analysis

 - Understand how host attributes influence booking performance

 - Analyze the impact of instant booking, SuperHost status, and identity verification

 - Evaluate cleanliness, response time, and pricing perception on guest trust

 - Identify high-performing cities, property types, and guest segments

 - Provide practical, data-driven recommendations to improve revenue and conversion

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

 - Total Sales: $170.3M

 - Total Bookings: 5.4M

 - Top Revenue Property Type: Entire Homes

 - Top Cities by Revenue: Cape Town, Bangkok

 - Instant Booking Contribution: Over 50% of total sales

## 🔍 Key Insights & Interpretations
### Revenue & Booking Behavior
   Sales remain relatively stable day-to-day, with slight peaks toward month-end, indicating last-minute booking behavior.
   Entire homes generate the highest revenue, confirming a strong guest preference for privacy.

### Pricing Perception
 - The majority of listings are rated “Excellent” for value, suggesting strong value-for-money perception.
  However, the sharp drop in mid-tier ratings may indicate rating inflation or weak pricing segmentation.
#### Insight: Guests may be choosing between “cheap” and “premium” with fewer attractive middle options.

### Cleanliness Performance
 - While “Outstanding” and “Excellent” dominate, over 94K listings are rated ‘Bad’ for cleanliness.

Poor cleanliness directly threatens trust, repeat bookings, and platform reputation.

### Host Trust & Verification
 - Most listings have verified identities, but 79K listings remain unverified, reducing guest confidence.
 - Nearly all hosts have profile pictures, which is a strong trust indicator.

### Host Responsiveness
 - A significant number of listings show “No Response”, which is a major conversion risk.
 - Faster response times clearly correlate with higher booking likelihood.

### Instant Booking Impact
 - Only 41% of listings offer instant booking, yet they generate more than half of total sales.
 - This highlights instant booking as a major revenue lever.

### Guest Capacity Trends
 - Listings accommodating 2 guests dominate bookings, indicating strong demand from couples and solo travelers.
 - Larger group listings exist but are less optimized and under-marketed.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase Instant Booking Adoption
Promote instant booking through incentives, education, and ranking advantages.

- Improve Cleanliness Standards
Introduce cleanliness improvement programs tied to visibility boosts or SuperHost eligibility.

- Optimize Host Responsiveness
Penalize non-responsive hosts and reward fast responders with priority placement.

- Expand Verified Identity Coverage
Encourage verification through trust badges and booking visibility incentives.

- Support SuperHost Growth
Create guided pathways to help high-performing hosts achieve SuperHost status.

- Refine Pricing Segmentation
Introduce clearer mid-tier pricing strategies to balance value perception.

- Focus on High-Performing Cities & Property Types
Double down on marketing and expansion in cities and listings that consistently outperform.
